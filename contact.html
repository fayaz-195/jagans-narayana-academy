How do I enquire about a new admission?

Fill in the enquiry form above, choose the class or programme, and continue to WhatsApp to send your enquiry.

Which programmes can I ask about?

Can I visit the campus?

Yes. Please contact the academy in advance to confirm a suitable visit time and get directions.

How can I ask about fees, transport or hostel information?

Choose the relevant enquiry type in the form and include your question in the message field. The academy can share current, confirmed details.
