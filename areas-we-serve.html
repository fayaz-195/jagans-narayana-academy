COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you visit

Does the academy provide transport from Balapur, Meerpet or other nearby areas?

No. Transport facility is not currently available. Please plan your own travel to the Badangpet campus.

Is the academy located in Meerpet, Nadergul or Adibatla?

No. The academy's campus is in Badangpet. The other localities are nearby areas from which families may contact the academy for admission enquiries.

Which programmes can I enquire about?

Schooling Classes 1–10, Intermediate MPC, BiPC, MEC and CEC, IIT-JEE, NEET, CA and CMA. Please contact the academy to confirm current availability and details.

How can I confirm fees and admission details?

Call +91 91334 44384 or use the online enquiry form. The academy team can share current information.
