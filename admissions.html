Admissions at our Badangpet campus

Families from Balapur, Meerpet, Almasguda, Nadergul and Adibatla can contact Jagan Sir's Narayana Academy to ask about current admissions, programme availability, eligibility and fees. The academy campus is in Badangpet; transport is not currently available. Please plan your own travel and call ahead before visiting.
